How CityFix Works

Bridging the gap between observant citizens and responsive city municipal teams in 4 simple steps.

1
Step 01

Capture

Take or upload a photo of a civic problem directly from your smartphone or browser in seconds.

2
Step 02

AI Detects

AI identifies the problem, estimates its severity level, and calculates an objective priority score.

3
Step 03

Locate

CityFix uses the location to pinpoint where the issue is, dropping an interactive map coordinate pin.

4
Step 04

Track

Authorities receive the report and citizens can track its progress until marked resolved with proof.
