Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect: CityFix processes issue photos, user-provided locations, and civic descriptions to facilitate infrastructure repair and transparency.

2. Public vs Private Data: Photos of public roadways, garbage, or civic infrastructure are displayed publicly on the city map. Personal contact details remain protected and visible only to assigned municipal departments.

3. AI Processing: Uploaded images are processed via computer vision models solely to identify civic defect categories and calculate severity scores.
