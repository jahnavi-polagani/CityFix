About CityFix

CityFix is an AI-powered civic technology platform created to empower citizens and municipal departments to collaborate on public infrastructure improvement.

For Citizens

Quick, friction-free civic problem reporting with automated location tagging, visual evidence analysis, and transparent status updates.

For City Authorities

Actionable, prioritized dashboards that classify incoming reports by severity and direct work orders to the correct municipal departments.
